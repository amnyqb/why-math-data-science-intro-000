# Acknowledgements

*Draft for the book. Names marked [confirm] are in the checklist at the end; remove the checklist before printing.*

This book is the work of one person, but it was made by many. A decade of learning system dynamics put me in debt to teachers, colleagues and family, and I want to thank them here.

## My teachers

**Professor Eiichi Yamaguchi** encouraged me to pursue a PhD. He taught me to think critically, and he advised me to study this field. Without that advice, and the confidence that came with it, I would not have started.

**Professor Kaoru Yamaguchi** was my first master in system dynamics. He introduced me to the field and to its community, and he gave me his time without counting it. He taught his course at Gifu University so that I could follow it, he opened his own home to host me, and he spent hours equipping me with the understanding that everything else in this book rests on. Above all he guided me onto the right path at the moment I most needed it. His fingerprints are on every chapter: the care for fundamentals, the insistence on seeing a system as a whole, and the patience to explain a hard idea until it is clear. I cannot thank him enough, and I am proud to be his student.

**Professor Yutaka Takahashi** tutored and guided me with great generosity during our meetings at Senshu University in Tokyo. His advice, his support and the time he gave me shaped how I think about models and how I teach them.

## Colleagues and friends of the field

I am indebted to many others whose discussions and comments improved this work, and whose own writing taught me the craft:

- **Professor John Sterman**, whose *Business Dynamics* set the standard for how to model and how to explain a model.
- **Professor Andrew Ford** [confirm], whose book on modelling the environment showed how clearly a model can speak to people outside the field.
- **Tom Fiddaman**, whose models, tools and open way of working I have learned from for years.
- **Dr. Santiago Blanco**.
- The **system dynamics faculty at Worcester Polytechnic Institute** [confirm names].
- **Professor Raafat Zaini**.
- **Professor Saeed Langarudi**.

Their engaging, fruitful discussions and valuable comments are the best part of working in this community. Any errors that remain are mine.

## My family

Finally, and most importantly, I thank my wife and my children. For the last decade or so they have been wonderful listeners to every complex talk and lecture I tried out on them, and a great support through all the long hours. This book is for them.
